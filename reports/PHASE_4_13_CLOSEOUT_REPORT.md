## 1. Executive Summary

This task resolves the root cause of ASTROVISION's elevated empirical false-positive rate ($	ext{FPR} = 9.00\%$ vs. nominal budget $lpha = 5.00\%$) and detector-stratified disparities.

Through systematic flag verification, crossmatching against local Gaia DR3 catalogs, and pixel-level morphological analysis, this audit establishes that:
1. **Official Flag Semantics (Task 4.13A):** The SPHEREx Level 2 flag bit 21 (`2097152`) is formally defined in FITS headers as `SOURCE` (or `MP_SOURCE`), with official comment `"Pixel mapped to a known source"` (generated by pipeline module `SSDC-MS-019 v3.5`). It marks spatial source apertures and covers **$62.3\%	ext{ to }79.7\%$ of all active detector pixels**. Bit 21 alone does not prove a source is a star.
2. **False-Positive Source Identity (Task 4.13B):** Crossmatching against local Gaia DR3 data identified 1 Probable Stellar Counterpart (`PILOT_001004`, $\Delta 	heta = 7.74''$) and 2 Ambiguous Counterparts. The remaining 42 trials lie outside local cached cone searches and are strictly designated **`UNVERIFIABLE`** in accordance with Mandatory Safety Rules 8–10.
3. **Decoupling of Center vs. Cutout Peak:** In **100% (45/45)** of the false-positive cutouts, the flux peak ($50.9	ext{--}2658.5\,	ext{MJy/sr}$) is **NOT** at the center aperture, but is located **$21.2	ext{ to }77.0	ext{ pixels}$ off-center** (mean distance: $48.25	ext{ pixels}$). Central flux is near background level (median $0.25\,	ext{MJy/sr}$).
4. **Benchmark Label Contamination (Task 4.13C):** Negative control coordinates were generated via unconstrained uniform random sampling without checking Level 2 flags or source proximity. In crowded fields, random coordinates routinely land on catalog source apertures.
5. **Reconciliation of 45 vs. 68 Metrics (Task 4.13F):** The 45 count represents the official evaluation protocol (unscaled anomaly map with subpixel centroid gate $r \le 2.5	ext{ px}$). The 68 count represents the planar normalization experiment protocol (nominal sky variance tensor passed, raw circle maximum without centroid gate).
6. **Detector 2 Calibration Safety (Task 4.13E):** Lowering $	au$ from $2536.30$ to $331.48$ restores point recovery from $8.33\%$ to $44.05\%$, but surges unvetted blind-field FPR to $22.62\%$. The production threshold remains strictly frozen until upstream star-masking is deployed.

## 10. Final Authoritative Verdict & Scientific Recommendation

### Authoritative Verdict: **`MIXED_SOURCE_AND_MODEL_FAILURE`**

**Scientific Justification:**  
The elevated false-positive rate is neither purely an artifact of catalog mislabeling nor purely a model architecture defect. Rather, it is a coupled failure:
1. **Source / Benchmark Contamination:** Negative control trials were sampled without Level 2 flag inspection or proximity buffers, placing evaluation apertures into field sectors where bright off-center catalog stars contaminate the local spatial background.
2. **Model Architecture & Convolutional Receptive Field Limitation:** The convolutional autoencoder cannot compress high-flux stellar cores off-center in the patch, resulting in large reconstruction error gradients that propagate across the receptive field into the target aperture.
3. **Threshold Skew in D2:** Calibration on Pointing 1 outliers inflated the D2 threshold, suppressing point recovery to 8.33%.

### Recommended Next Scientific Action:
- Implement an upstream **Catalog-Aware Star Masking Module** (`is_known_gaia_star` via MOTION-X / FUSION-X) to dynamically mask known stars during spatial inference, preventing off-center stellar reconstruction error leakage.
- Once star masking is operational, re-calibrate detector thresholds on clean, unmasked calibration sky to restore D2 recovery without inflating the false-alarm rate.
