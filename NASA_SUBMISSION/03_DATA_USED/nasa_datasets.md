# NASA Datasets & Space Mission Resources Used

**Challenge Track:** Planet X: Moving Object Finder  
**Target Mission:** NASA SPHEREx (Spectro-Photometer for the History of the Universe, Epoch of Reionization, and Ices Explorer)  

---

## 1. NASA Mission Datasets

### A. NASA SPHEREx Level-2 Data Products
- **Provider:** NASA / Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL) / IPAC (Infrared Processing and Analysis Center) at Caltech.
- **Data Release Version:** SPHEREx Quick Release 2 / 3 (QR2/QR3) simulation datasets and Level-2 pre-flight data pipelines.
- **Instrument Characteristics:**
  - Full-sky spectral survey spanning $0.75\ \mu\text{m}$ to $5.00\ \mu\text{m}$.
  - 102 continuous near-infrared spectral channels divided across 6 linear-variable filter (LVF) detector arrays.
  - Spatial resolution: $6.2^{\prime\prime}$ per pixel.
  - Survey Cadence: Complete all-sky coverage every 6 months.
- **Data Format:** Flexible Image Transport System (FITS) multi-extension files containing:
  - Extension 1: Primary science image array (Flux in MJy/sr).
  - Extension 2: Variance / uncertainty map.
  - Extension 3: Data quality bitmask (cosmic rays, saturation, bad pixels).
  - World Coordinate System (WCS) headers with TAN/SIP distortion coefficients.

### B. Access & Provenance
- **Repository:** NASA/IPAC Infrared Science Archive (IRSA) — `https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu`
- **Application Role in SPECTRA-X:**
  - Serves as the primary observational input format for the ASTROVISION spatial CNN and SPECTRA 102-channel masked autoencoder.
  - Used to benchmark the intra-visit dither separation logic against genuine multi-day orbital displacement baselines.

## 2. Limitations & Scientific Safeguards

1. **Intra-Visit Dither Cadence:**
   - In standard SPHEREx survey operations, consecutive exposures in the same field are acquired with small spatial dithers separated by approximately 5 minutes ($\Delta t \approx 0.0035$ days).
   - Apparent centroid shifts observed across intra-visit frames represent spacecraft pointing dithers, NOT interplanetary orbital velocity.
   - **Safeguard in SPECTRA-X:** The MOTION-X engine programmatically flags any observation pair with baseline $\Delta t < 0.05$ days ($\approx 72$ minutes) with a prominent `Intra-Visit Dither` warning, preventing false orbital discovery claims.

2. **Pre-Commissioning Nature:**
   - Current datasets utilize pre-flight calibration products and high-fidelity simulated Level-2 sky scenes generated by the SPHEREx Science Team.
   - The SPECTRA-X architecture is format-compatible with future live on-orbit Level-2 data releases upon public availability.
