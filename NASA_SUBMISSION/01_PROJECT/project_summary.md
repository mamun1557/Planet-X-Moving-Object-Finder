# SPECTRA-X: Executive Project Summary

**Challenge Track:** Planet X: Moving Object Finder  
**Team:** VU_VioNux  
**Event:** NASA International Space Apps Challenge  

---

### Project Summary (2–4 Sentences)

SPECTRA-X is an input-driven, human-in-the-loop astronomical analysis platform engineered to discover moving objects, transient phenomena, and spectral anomalies across multi-epoch infrared surveys, specifically NASA's SPHEREx mission. By combining a 5-module AI architecture (ASTROVISION, SPECTRA, CHRONOS-X, MOTION-X, and FUSION-X) with rigorous WCS astrometric motion solvers, the system automates candidate screening across 102 spectral channels while enforcing strict intra-visit dither baseline safeguards. Users provide their own astronomical datasets via standard FITS, CSV, or parquet formats into isolated, ephemeral sessions, inspecting optical blink comparisons, full-resolution spectral energy distributions, and verifiable displacement vectors before flagging candidates for astronomical confirmation.
