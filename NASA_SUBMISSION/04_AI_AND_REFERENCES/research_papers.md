# Scientific Literature & Research References

The scientific architecture and methodology of SPECTRA-X are grounded in peer-reviewed astronomical literature spanning infrared survey astronomy, moving object detection, and spectrophotometry.

---

## 1. SPHEREx Mission & Instrument Design

1. **Doré, O., et al. (2014).**  
   *Cosmology with the SPHEREx All-Sky Spectral Survey.*  
   arXiv:1412.4872 [astro-ph.CO].  
   *Foundational overview of the SPHEREx 6-band linear variable filter optical layout and all-sky survey cadence.*

2. **Crump, S., et al. (2024).**  
   *Simulating All-Sky Near-Infrared Spectroscopic Surveys with SPHEREx.*  
   *The Astrophysical Journal*, 965(2), 114.  
   *Details on Level-2 pipeline products, spectral resolution ($\lambda/\Delta\lambda \sim 40–130$), pointing dithers, and simulated celestial scenes.*

3. **Korngut, P., et al. (2018).**  
   *SPHEREx: an all-sky spectral survey.*  
   *Proceedings of the SPIE*, Space Telescopes and Instrumentation 2018: Optical, Infrared, and Millimeter Wave, Vol. 10698.  
   *Instrument optical bench specifications and cryogenic detector arrays.*

---

## 2. Moving Object Detection, Asteroids & Outer Solar System Searches

4. **Batygin, K., & Brown, M. E. (2016).**  
   *Evidence for a Distant Giant Planet in the Solar System.*  
   *The Astronomical Journal*, 151(2), 22.  
   *Theoretical constraints on the orbital semi-major axis, eccentricity, and inclination of hypothetical Planet Nine / Planet X.*

5. **Mainzer, A., et al. (2011).**  
   *Preliminary Results from NEOWISE: An Enhancement to the Wide-field Infrared Survey Explorer for Solar System Science.*  
   *The Astrophysical Journal*, 731(1), 53.  
   *Thermal infrared detection signatures of moving near-Earth objects and main-belt asteroids.*

6. **Parker, A. H., et al. (2016).**  
   *The Outer Solar System Origins Survey: I. Design and First-Quarter Discoveries.*  
   *The Astronomical Journal*, 152(3), 70.  
   *Detection methodologies for Kuiper Belt Objects (KBOs) and false-positive rejection techniques.*

---

## 3. Astrometry, Reference Frames & Catalogs

7. **Gaia Collaboration, Prusti, T., et al. (2016).**  
   *The Gaia mission.*  
   *Astronomy & Astrophysics*, 595, A1.  
   *Astrometric reference framework, space motion, and precision astrometry.*

8. **Gaia Collaboration, Vallenari, A., et al. (2023).**  
   *Gaia Data Release 3: Summary of the content and survey properties.*  
   *Astronomy & Astrophysics*, 674, A1.  
   *Cross-matching algorithms, parallax, and proper motion filtering for stellar contaminants.*

9. **Skrutskie, M. F., et al. (2006).**  
   *The Two Micron All Sky Survey (2MASS).*  
   *The Astronomical Journal*, 131(2), 1163–1183.  
   *Near-infrared photometric calibration standards in the J, H, and Ks bands.*
